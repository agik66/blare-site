PRIVACY POLICY

Effective 13 November 2026 · applies to the Blare iOS app and the Blare web player

Blare is an internet-radio player. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no tracking. We do not store or sell personal data, and we keep no record of what you play or search for. This page lists everything the software keeps and everyone it talks to, so you can check the claim.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

The following is stored only locally (iOS app: on the phone; web: in your browser's local storage) and is never sent to us:

  • starred stations, listening history and the MIX filter (the iOS app can switch the history off and erase it: Settings → HISTORY),
  • settings: theme, visualiser mode, equaliser presets, layout options,
  • a cached copy of the house-ad list and of the station catalog,
  • on iOS, a cached flag saying whether the ad-free purchase is owned.

Deleting the app, or clearing site data in the browser, removes all of it. There is no export to a server and no sync.

ADVERTISING

The boxes marked AD show the developer's own products (“house ads”). They are plain images listed in a public JSON file; showing them sends no data anywhere except the request that downloads that file and the image.

On the web player the manifest can additionally enable Google AdSense units. When that is on, Google serves the ad and may use cookies or similar identifiers under its own policy (policies.google.com/technologies/ads). When it is off, no Google code is loaded. The iOS app does not contain AdSense or any other ad network.

On iOS a one-time purchase, “Remove ads”, hides every ad box. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple; we receive no name, e-mail or payment detail, only the anonymous receipt Apple gives the app.

SIRI AND SHORTCUTS

Siri and Shortcuts commands (“play a station”, “play my favourites”, “next”, “stop”) run on the device through Apple's App Intents. Station names you speak are processed by Apple as part of Siri; the app only receives the chosen station.

MICROPHONE, LOCATION, CONTACTS

None. The visualiser analyses the audio of the stream being played, not the microphone. The station globe uses the station's published coordinates, not your location.

CHILDREN

Blare is not directed at children and keeps no data about anyone, of any age.

YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR)

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase. Everything the software keeps is under your control on your own device. If you believe we do hold something, write to the address on the Support page and we will answer within 30 days.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date. The app links to this page.
