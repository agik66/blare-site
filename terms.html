TERMS OF USE

Effective 12 November 2026

WHAT BLARE IS

Blare is a player. It tunes publicly available internet-radio streams and shows what the station itself reports as playing. Blare does not host, record, store or redistribute any audio. Each stream is delivered by the station's own server directly to your device.

STATIONS AND CONTENT

The station directory is compiled from the community project Radio Browser and from public station metadata. The stations, their programmes, their names and logos belong to their operators. A stream may be off-air, geo-blocked, changed or removed by its operator at any time; Blare cannot guarantee that any station plays. If you operate a station and want it changed or removed from the directory, contact us via the Support page and we will act on the next catalog update.

YOUR USE

You may use Blare for personal, non-commercial listening. You must not use it to circumvent a station's own restrictions, to record streams where that is not permitted, or in any way that violates law or the station's terms.

PURCHASES

“Remove ads” on iOS is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase sold and processed by Apple under the App Store terms. It hides ad boxes in the app on every device signed in with the same Apple ID; use “Restore” in Settings on a new device. Refunds are handled by Apple. The web player has no purchase and its ad boxes stay.

ADVERTISING

Boxes marked AD show the developer's own products and, on the web when enabled, paid units. Ads are never inserted into the audio and never interrupt playback.
