BLARE
STANDBY

INTERNET RADIO, TERMINAL STYLE

Blare is a radio player for iPhone and iPad. It tunes more than 8,000 stations, shows what the station says is playing, and draws a real spectrum of the audio you hear. Nothing is faked: when a station publishes no title, Blare says so.

WHAT IT DOES

  • PLAY — the station, its live title and a 16-band FFT visualiser in six modes.
  • FIND — type an artist, see which stations have been playing them.
  • MIX — an auto-tuner that hops stations, optionally inside a genre or country.
  • LIB — the catalog by genre and country, with a globe of station positions.
  • HIST — what you heard, where.
  • Siri and Shortcuts, CarPlay, a ten-band equaliser with your own presets, eleven themes.

PRICE

Free. Small boxes show the developer's own products; a one-time purchase removes them. No accounts, no tracking — see the privacy policy.
