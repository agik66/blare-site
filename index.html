~/RADIO/BLARE · INTERNET RADIO, TERMINAL STYLE

A radio that shows you the signal.

Blare tunes 8,000+ internet stations, prints what the station says is playing, and draws a real spectrum of the audio you hear. Nothing is faked: when a station publishes no title, Blare says so. This page runs the same player as the iPhone app.

          [01]

          ./features

          WEB · iOS

          PLAY

          The station, its live title, and a 16-band FFT of the stream in six visualiser modes: matrix, bars, mirror, dots, scope, ASCII. The caption tells you when a stream cannot be measured.

          WEB · iOS

          FIND

          Type an artist and see which stations have been playing them, built from what stations reported over time. The iOS app adds a live tier that watches the station you are on.

          WEB · iOS

          MIX

          An auto-tuner. It hops stations, optionally inside a genre or a country, and tells you why it picked each one.

          WEB · iOS

          LIB

          The catalog by genre and country, with flags, and a globe of every station that has a coordinate. Drag to spin, pinch to zoom.

          WEB · iOS

          HIST

          What you heard and where. Save a track so it survives the rolling log; saved tracks export as a playlist.

          WEB · iOS

          THEMES

          Nine bundled palettes from Tokyo Night to Rosé Pine Dawn, four more compiled in, generators, and a custom theme you can share as a code.

          iOS ONLY

          EQUALISER

          Ten bands, ±12 dB, presets and your own saved curves. The web player has no EQ; browsers do not expose the decoded stream the same way.

          iOS ONLY

          SIRI · SHORTCUTS

          “Play Rádio Rock in Blare”, “Play my favourites”, “Next station”, “Stop”. On iOS 16.4 and newer.

          WEB · iOS

          SHARE A STATION LIST

          Copy your starred stations or your theme as a Blare-code and paste it on another device. No account involved.

          ./stations

          —STATIONS IN THE CATALOG
          —COUNTRIES
          —PUBLISH “NOW PLAYING”
          —PLAY ON THIS HTTPS PAGE

          The directory is compiled from Radio Browser and public station metadata. Stations go off-air and move; the player shows the failure it got from the server instead of guessing. Browsers refuse http:// streams on an https page, so a part of the catalog plays only in the iOS app until a proxy exists.

          [03]

          ./the-app

          Blare for iPhone and iPad runs on iOS 15 and newer, down to the iPhone 7 and the iPad Air 2. It is free; small boxes carry the developer's own products, and a one-time purchase removes them. The App Store listing is submitted and not yet live — this line changes to a link the day it is.
