WHAT TO INCLUDE IN A BUG REPORT

  • device and iOS version (Settings → General → About), or browser and OS on the web,
  • the station's name, if the problem is one station,
  • what you did, what you expected and what happened instead,
  • a screenshot if the screen looked wrong.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A station does not play. Stations go off-air, move their stream or block countries; the app shows the failure it got from the server. Try another station in the same genre. If a well-known station stays dead for days, mail its name and we will fix the catalog entry.

The title line says “no title”. That station does not publish what is playing. Blare only shows what the stream sends; it does not guess. The LIB screen filter “publishes now playing” lists the stations that do.

The visualiser shows a pattern, not the music. On the web, browsers only let the page analyse streams whose server allows cross-origin reads; other streams get a generated pattern and the caption says so. In the iOS app the spectrum is a real FFT of the audio whenever the caption under it says LIVE SPECTRUM; on a station the app cannot decode itself, or with ANALYSE AUDIO switched off in Settings, it draws a pattern and the caption says GENERATED PATTERN.

I paid for Remove ads and the ads are back. Open Settings → ADS → Restore purchases while online and signed in with the Apple ID that bought it. If that fails, contact Apple Support for the purchase itself, or mail us with the date of purchase.

FIND does not know an artist. FIND indexes what stations have reported playing over time. An artist nobody in the catalog has played is not there yet.

How do I switch off or erase my listening history? In the iOS app: Settings → HISTORY. LISTENING HISTORY off stops the log; CLEAR HISTORY deletes it, saved tracks included. Nothing in that log leaves the device unless you export your saved tracks yourself.

I run a radio station. See the next section.

STATION REMOVAL AND COPYRIGHT CONTACT

Blare plays publicly available internet-radio streams. Station details come from Radio Browser, a community directory. Blare does not host, record, rebroadcast or modify any audio; the app connects the listener's device to the broadcaster's own server.

If you operate a station and want its entry changed or removed, or you hold rights in material being carried, write to support@syenit.eu with the station name and, if you have it, the stream URL. Listings are corrected or removed on request, without argument. A removal takes effect with the next app update; the same address is in the app under Settings → REPORT A STATION.
